# Flippity v1 decisions

## D1. The name is Flippity

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** What is the game called?

**Options:** Many lists: Flapjack, Dayside, Petalflip, Turnabout, Flick, Flank, Pebblet, Skipstone, Pondpip, Ploppet, Lilyflip, Flipplop, Flippity and others.

**Choice:** Flippity.

**Reason:** The owner wanted a playful name with "flip" in it. Flippity was the one they liked.

## D2. Bots and pass and play, no daily

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** What is the main way to play?

**Options:** Vs a bot with a daily puzzle, vs a bot only, a puzzle path, solo vs the tide.

**Choice:** Three bot levels (Sleepy, Steady, Sharp) and pass and play. No daily puzzle.

**Reason:** The owner liked the three bots and pass and play the most. A full game is too long to be a daily.

## D3. The pieces are pebbles

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** What are the pieces?

**Options:** Lily pads, pebbles, shells, frogs.

**Choice:** Pebbles: smooth stones, light on one side and dark on the other.

**Reason:** The owner's pick. Pebbles fit the soft clay look, and the light and dark sides read well in every theme.

## D4. No board unlocks

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** Do the boards (shallow pond, mossy rock, sandy shore, wooden dock) become shop unlocks?

**Options:** Drop the boards, keep one pond board, keep all four as unlocks.

**Choice:** Drop the boards. The board is a clay tray in the colors of the theme. A small ripple shows when a pebble lands.

**Reason:** The cozy kit themes already change the whole look. Scene boards would be a second set of looks, and they need detailed art that does not match the flat clay style.

## D5. The player picks the board size

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** How big is the board?

**Options:** 8×8 only, 6×6 only, 6×6 with 8×8 as an unlock, a pick before each game. 12×12 was also asked about.

**Choice:** A pick before each game: 6×6, 8×8 or 10×10.

**Reason:** The owner wants short and long games. On a 375px phone a 12×12 square is about 28px, which is too small. A 10×10 square is about 34px. Even sizes keep the four start pebbles in the center.

## D6. The currency is Pips, earned by good moves

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** How does the player earn, and what is the currency called?

**Options:** Unlock by beating each bot, by win streaks, by total wins, or earn a currency in the game. For the name: Coins (as in Wordkin and Hueway), or a Flippity name.

**Choice:** Pips. Big flips, double lines, corners and wins earn Pips. Pass and play earns nothing.

**Reason:** The owner wants good moves to pay, not only wins. The rule for all games: Coins by default, and a game's own name only when one fits well (Kerplunk has Plunks). Pips fits Flippity.

## D7. The shop sells themes, pebble styles and sounds

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** What does the shop sell?

**Options:** Pebble styles, sounds, both, or only themes.

**Choice:** Both: the cozy kit themes, four pebble styles and four sound packs.

**Reason:** The owner's pick. Pebble styles and sounds are what the player sees and hears on every move.

## D8. Help: one free hint and undo, move dots in Settings

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** What help does the player get?

**Options:** Move dots and undo, move dots only, move dots and paid hints, all three.

**Choice:** One free hint and one free undo per game. More cost Pips. Move dots are a setting, off by default, in the accessibility part of Settings.

**Reason:** The owner's design. Free help is there, but too much help costs something. Move dots help players who need them, without making the game easy for everyone.

## D9. Bloom comes later

**Date:** 2026-11-27

**Question:** Which twists go into 1.0?

**Options:** Odd boards, rocks, anchors, bloom, wraparound, combo flip, solo vs the tide.

**Choice:** None in 1.0. Bloom is the twist the owner liked. It may come in a later version.

**Reason:** Build the main game first.
