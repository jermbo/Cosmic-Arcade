Each color has two dots. Draw a line to join them. Lines can't cross, and every square on the board must be filled. Tint, a little drop of paint, keeps you company and takes on the color you draw.

## Modes

| Mode      | What you get |
| --------- | ------------ |
| Today     | One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Monday is a small 5×5 board, and it grows through the week up to a 9×9 board on Sunday. |
| This week | Play the earlier days of the week you missed. Only today's puzzle counts for the streak. |
| Practice  | Endless puzzles at Easy (5×5), Medium (6×6), Hard (7×7), Expert (8×8) or Master (9×9). |
| Clock run | Endless. Race the clock: each solved board adds time, and the boards grow from 4×4. Your score is the number of boards. |
| Road trip | Endless and calm. One red line is the road: it leaves each board on the right and starts the next one on the left. A map shows the whole trip. |

Every puzzle has exactly one answer.

## Shop

Each solved puzzle earns coins: more for bigger boards, no hints, perfect lines (each color drawn in one go), a new best time and your daily streak. Clock runs and road trip stops earn coins too. Spend them on themes (light and dark), line styles, dot styles and sound packs.

## Play offline

Hueway works offline after the first visit. Tap **Install** on the title screen to keep it as an app, or on an iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Controls

- **Touch or mouse:** drag from a dot to its match. Drag back to shorten a line. Draw over a line to cut it. Tap a dot to clear its line.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Space or Enter picks up and drops a line, Z undo, H hint, Esc back.

**Style:** Soft (soft clay) or Bold (ink outlines and hard shadows), a free setting. The themes are shared with the arcade's other cozy puzzle games.
