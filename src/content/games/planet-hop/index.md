The Star Lighthouse has gone dark. Pip, a sheep astronaut with a lantern, hops across a chain of tiny planets. Each planet has its own gravity, so you can run all the way around it.

## How to play

- Gather stardust to charge the launch pad. When it glows, follow the light beam and step on it to fly to the next planet.
- **Clover**, the garden planet: pink blossoms bounce you high. Hold jump on one to go higher.
- **Rime**, the ice-crystal planet: the ground is slippery. Jump on frost mites to pop them. Each one leaves a stardust.
- **Cinder**, the lava planet: vents glow before they erupt. Keep off the lava, then light the lighthouse.
- Pip's lantern holds three sparks. Lose them all and you go back to the landing spot. You keep your stardust.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad | Touch |
| ------ | ------------------ | ------- | ----- |
| Move   | WASD or arrows     | Left stick | Left thumb |
| Camera | Mouse              | Right stick | Drag on the right |
| Jump   | Space, hold for higher | A, hold for higher | JUMP button |
| Pause  | Esc or P           | Start   | Pause button |

On a phone, play in landscape. Settings has camera speed, camera assist, and a reduce-motion option.
