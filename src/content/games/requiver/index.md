You are a lone archer on the left of the field. Waves come from the right. Cross three lands on a branching map and beat the boss at the end of each, before the sun sets.

## How to play

- **Shoot**: drag back anywhere, aim the arc, let go. Keyboard: the **arrows** aim, hold **Space** and let go. Gamepad: the **left stick** aims, hold **A** or the right trigger.
- **Hit zones**: a head shot kills in one hit. A body shot takes two. Knock helmets and chest plates off first.
- **Combo**: kill streaks fill the meter. A miss resets the streak. A full meter gives **Arrow Rain**. Fill it mostly with head shots for the **Crown Shot** instead.
- **Foes**: flyers come high, shields block flat shots (lob over them), throwers throw rocks you can shoot down, splitters burst in two.
- **Bosses**: each has a glowing weak spot. Hit it during a wind-up to break the attack.
- **Health** carries over for the whole run. Rest, shops and some boosts heal you.

## Between runs

Every run pays **Feathers**, win or lose. Spend them in the upgrade tree: more health, a faster pull, a longer aim arc, more boost slots, new bows, new boosts and new events.

Best for a phone held sideways. Tap **Install** on the title screen (or Add to Home Screen on iPhone) to keep it as an app. It plays offline after the first load.

All art, sound, music and text are original. The sound and music are made in code.
