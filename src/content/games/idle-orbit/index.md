Tap a planet to mine by hand. Then open it and build a small factory: drills on ore, belts to move it, a smelter to make bars, and a launch pad. Cargo ships fly it home and sell it, even while you are away.

## How to play

- Tap a planet on the space map to mine. Open it to build.
- Put drills on glowing ore. Drag to lay belts to the launch pad.
- Tap a machine to upgrade, turn or remove it.
- Follow the **Next** bar at the top. Unlock six planets, then go nova for stardust.
- Keyboard: **1–8** tools, **R** turn, **Esc** back, **Tab** map / planet.
- Works offline after the first load (PWA).
