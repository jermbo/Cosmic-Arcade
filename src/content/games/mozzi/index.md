A small logic puzzle with a new mosaic window every day. Each window is a 4×4 grid of tiles. Give every tile a number and a color. Mozzi, a creature made of four tiles, watches you play and gives the hints.

## Rules

- Each row and each column has every number (1–4) once and every color once.
- Each number + color pair is used once in the whole window.
- There are no boxes, unlike Sudoku.

Some parts are set in lead from the start: they are the clues.

## Modes

| Mode      | What you get |
| --------- | ------------ |
| Today     | One window a day, the same for everyone. Monday is gentle, the weekend is tough. |
| This week | Play the earlier days of the week you missed. Only today's window counts for the streak. |
| Practice  | Endless windows at Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. |

## Shop

Each window you mend earns coins: more for harder levels, no hints, a new best time and your daily streak. Spend them on themes (light and dark), tile styles and sound packs.

## Play offline

Mozzi works offline after the first visit. Tap **Install** on the title screen to keep it as an app, or on an iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Controls

- **Touch or mouse:** tap a tile, then tap a number and a color at the bottom of the screen.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, 1–4 numbers, Q W E R colors, N notes, Backspace erase, Z / Y undo and redo, H hint, P pairs, Esc pause.
- **Gamepad:** D-pad moves, A next number, X next color, Y notes, B erase, LB / RB undo and redo, Select hint, Start pause.

## Hints

A hint goes in three steps: where to look, why it must be so, and what goes there. Mozzi always checks for a wrong tile first. Every window can be finished with hints alone, with no guessing.
