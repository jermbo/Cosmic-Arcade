You are Kestrel Unit 09, an operator jacked into a megacorp's network. Pick a layer, descend four floors, and kill the root daemon. You have no health. You have **trace**: hit 100 and you are booted.

## How to play

- Pick a node on the map. Paths only go down.
- In a fight, play cards with your 3 energy. Attacks hit the first daemon in line.
- Loud cards add **noise** to trace. **Mask** blocks daemon attacks for one turn.
- Breach the gate at the end of floors 1 to 3 to shed 35% trace.
- Jack out from the map to keep 40% of your cycles. Clear the layer to keep all of them.
- Keyboard: **1-7** play a card, **E** end turn, **D** deck.

## Layers

| Layer       | Note                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| The Gallery | Soft ICE. Many crawlers.              |
| The Sump    | Lone heavy daemons.                   |
| The Watch   | Mixed patrols.                        |
| The Purge   | Lockjaw packs.                        |
| The Core    | Hunters everywhere. Elites are common. |

Clear a layer to open the next one. This version keeps progress only while the tab is open.
