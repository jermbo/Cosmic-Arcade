You get one car and one try. Watch the traffic, aim, and launch into the junction. After the first hit, time slows down while you push your wreck into more traffic. Once in each run you can blow up your own car. Each wreck pays money, and wrecks in quick succession raise the multiplier.

## How to play

- **Aim and launch.** Pick your line, then launch. You can steer a little on the way in.
- **Aftertouch.** After the first hit, push your wreck around in slow motion. The meter is short and does not refill.
- **Crashbreaker.** One blast for each run. Use it when the most vehicles are close.
- **Bonus.** Buses and tankers pay the most. Tankers and red barrels explode.
- Six junctions, each with its own goals. Reach one goal to open the next junction.

## Controls

- Keyboard and mouse: **← →** or the mouse to aim, **Space** or a click to launch, arrows or **W A S D** to push, **Space** for the Crashbreaker, **R** to retry, **Esc** to pause.
- Gamepad: left stick to aim, steer and push, **A** to launch and for the Crashbreaker, **Y** to retry, **Start** to pause.
- Phone, in landscape: drag to aim, steer and push, and tap the buttons on the screen.
