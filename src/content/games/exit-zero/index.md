Tuesday, 4:52 PM. One step too many on the stairs, and you fall through the floor of the world. You land in the maintenance level: yellow halls that hum, a dead office, a breaker corridor, a flooded utility room. Somewhere down here is a door marked EXIT 0. Zero means clean. Zero means it worked.

There is no combat. Explore, read the notes the last person left, fix what is broken, and get the gate open. Two things down here can end you, and both sleep until you wake them.

## How to play

- **Find things.** Keys, tools and notes are lying around. Notes hold codes and warnings. Read them again in your pockets.
- **Fix things.** The main box in the breaker room needs a wrench and a fuse. Hold the button to work.
- **Walk.** Something tall listens for running feet. Sprinting is loud. Splashing is loud. Switches are loud.
- **Mind the switches.** Not every label tells the truth, and the flood room does not like the pump.
- Checkpoints save in your browser. Continue picks up at the last one.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
| ------ | ------------------ | ------- |
| Move | WASD | Left stick |
| Look | Mouse | Right stick |
| Sprint (loud) | Shift | LT or L3 |
| Use, pick up | E or click (hold for repairs) | A or RT |
| Pockets and notes | Tab or I | Y |
| Flashlight | F | X |
| First or third person | V | Back |
| Pause | Esc | Start |

Settings has volume, look speed, field of view, first or third person, resolution (240p to 480p), the CRT filter and vertex wobble.

The slice is one level, about 15 to 20 minutes. Desktop only for now.
