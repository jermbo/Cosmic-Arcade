A UFO fleet wants your Heart Crystal. Build towers along the road and stop them before they reach it.

## How to play

- Pick a green tile next to the road and build a tower.
- Press **Start wave** when you are ready. The arrows on the road show where the UFOs go.
- Earn gold for each UFO you shoot down. Spend it on more towers and upgrades.
- At level 3, each tower picks one of two branches. The choice is final.
- Win a map to unlock the next one. Keep all 20 lives for 3 stars.

## Towers

| Tower         | Job        | Note                                 |
| ------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Ballista      | Pierce     | Bolts fly through a line of UFOs.    |
| Cannon        | Splash     | Shells burst and hit a crowd.        |
| Frost Crystal | Slow       | Pulses slow every UFO nearby.        |
| Catapult      | Long range | Heavy rocks. Cannot hit high flyers. |

## Enemies

| UFO        | Type                                             |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Scout      | Fast, low health.                                |
| Hauler     | Slow, lots of health.                            |
| Skimmer    | Flies high, straight to the crystal.             |
| Splitter   | Breaks into three small UFOs.                    |
| Mothership | The boss at the end of each map.                 |

## Controls

| Action         | Mouse and keyboard | Gamepad      | Touch        |
| -------------- | ------------------ | ------------ | ------------ |
| Select a tile  | Click, or arrows and Enter | Stick and A | Tap |
| Build          | 1 to 4             | A in the menu | Tap in the menu |
| Upgrade / sell | U / S              | X / Y        | Tower panel  |
| Next wave      | Space              | Select       | Button       |
| Speed          | F                  | RT           | Button       |
| Pause          | P or Esc           | Start        | Button       |

On a phone, play in landscape.
