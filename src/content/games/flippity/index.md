Put a pebble down so it traps a line of the other color, and the whole line flips to yours. When nobody can move, the player with more pebbles wins.

## Opponents

| Opponent      | What they are like |
| ------------- | ------------------ |
| Sleepy        | Half awake. Plays almost any move. A gentle first game. |
| Steady        | Thinks one move ahead and loves corners. |
| Sharp         | Looks many moves ahead and plays the end of the game exactly. |
| Pass and play | Two people, one phone. Take turns. |

Pick a board before each game: 6×6 for a quick game, 8×8 for the classic, or 10×10 for a long one. Against a bot you can go first (dark) or second (light).

## Pips and the shop

Good moves against a bot earn Pips: big flips, two or more lines at once, and corners. A win pays more against a stronger bot. Spend Pips on themes (light and dark), pebble styles (River stone, Sea glass, Marble, Gem) and sound packs (Plip, Clack, Chime, Knock). Pass and play earns no Pips.

## Help

Each game has one free hint and one free undo. More cost a few Pips. Move dots, which show every legal move, are a setting under Accessibility.

## Play offline

Flippity works offline after the first visit. Tap **Install** on the title screen to keep it as an app, or on an iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Controls

- **Touch or mouse:** tap a square to put a pebble there.
- **Keyboard:** arrows move, Enter or Space plays, Z undo, H hint, Esc back.

**Style:** Soft (soft clay) or Bold (ink outlines and hard shadows), a free setting. The themes are shared with the arcade's other cozy puzzle games.
